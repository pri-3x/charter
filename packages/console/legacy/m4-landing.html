Runtime authorization · tamper-evident audit

The control plane for what your AI agents are allowed to do.

Charter sits between an agent and the tools it calls — a policy gate that answers ALLOW · DENY · ESCALATE on every consequential action, fused with an append-only, hash-chained, Merkle-checkpointed ledger.

No verdict is ever returned before its ledger entry is durably committed. Every decision is evidence — independently verifiable, down to the exact byte.

policy gate

Versioned policy engine

First-match rules with matchers, per-agent scope + autonomy caps, and stateful count/sum limits — velocity and anti-structuring. Every check returns a full rule trace.

audit ledger

Hash-chained & append-only

Each entry is SHA-256 chained per tenant over RFC 8785 canonical JSON — INSERT-only at the role level and enforced by a trigger. Even a superuser can't quietly rewrite it.

human-in-the-loop

Approvals over Telegram

ESCALATE opens a hold; an approver taps Approve or Reject; self-approval is refused; past-TTL holds expire fail-closed. Every decision is its own ledger entry.

verifiable

Independent verifier

A standalone CLI re-derives the chain and signed Merkle checkpoints from scratch — 1M entries in seconds. It proves the ledger untampered, or names the exact record that wasn't.
